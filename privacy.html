Summary

CreatorCue is a teleprompter camera. Your scripts and recordings are created and kept on your phone. Our server, on Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia, stores what's needed for your account and recording allowance, Premium checks, the files you choose to put in My Vault, and your support conversations. We don't sell personal data, we don't use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't use your files or messages for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

This policy applies to the CreatorCue Android app and this website. CreatorCue is published by NextFrame Studio, an individual developer based in Australia.

Your account

  • You sign in with Google, or with an email address and password, through Firebase Authentication (a Google service).
  • To record on the free plan, your email address must be verified.
  • Firebase Authentication stores your email address, your sign-in method and account dates. Where you sign in with Google, it may also hold your display name and profile image link. It is operated by Google, which may process this data in countries other than Australia.

What stays on your phone

  • Scripts, prompt settings and preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and aren't uploaded. You can export a backup to a file you choose.
  • Recordings are saved to your phone's Movies/CreatorCue folder, so they appear in your Gallery. They aren't uploaded unless you add them to My Vault.
  • Premium status: the app keeps the time Google Play last confirmed your Premium subscription, so you can keep recording offline for up to 72 hours.

Where our server data is stored

Our database and file storage are on Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia, and our server functions also run in Sydney.

Free recording allowance and Premium checks

Why we store it: so every account gets the same free allowance, and it can't be reused by creating new accounts.

What the app sends when you start a recording: a request ID, a one-way scrambled identifier for your device, and your time zone.

What our server stores for this:

  • a scrambled key made from your email address with a secret key, which isn't your email address;
  • your monthly count of recordings, including any bonus recordings and returned (failed) recordings;
  • when your account was first seen and created;
  • your account time zone;
  • the scrambled device keys used with your account that month.

These quota records don't contain your email address or your account ID. A recording that fails, or stops before any usable video is saved, is returned to your allowance.

If you subscribe to Premium:

  • the app sends the Google Play purchase token to our server, which asks Google Play whether the subscription is active;
  • we keep the token while it is linked to your account, together with the subscription's status, plan, end date and whether it renews automatically, so we can check renewals. Our server asks Google Play again when the paid period ends and about once a day while the subscription runs or hasn't yet ended, so changes such as a cancellation, refund or plan change are reflected;
  • Google Play receives a one-way scrambled identifier derived from your account, which can't be turned back into your email address or name;
  • a subscription belongs to the account it was bought with and can't be moved to another account.

Google Play processes payments. We never receive your card or bank details.

How long we keep these records: they are deleted automatically no more than 12 months after they were last updated.

Bonus recordings from ads

  • On the free plan, if you tap "Watch an ad for +1 recording", Google AdMob shows a rewarded ad.
  • AdMob tells our server directly that the ad was completed, using a short-lived ticket linked to your scrambled account key, and our server then adds one bonus recording.
  • You can earn up to 5 bonus recordings a day. A bonus lasts until the end of the month.
  • Premium has no ads.

My Vault

My Vault is optional private cloud storage in your CreatorCue account.

  • What is uploaded: only the photos and videos you choose in Android's photo picker. CreatorCue doesn't get access to your other photos.
  • What we store: each file, plus its type, size and date, in your CreatorCue account.
  • Who can see it: other users can't see your files. Authorized CreatorCue administrators can view them only to provide technical support, to keep CreatorCue secure, to handle reports, or to meet legal obligations, and we don't use them for anything else. Every view is logged with its reason. Administrators can't change or delete your files.
  • Limits: 2,000 files per account, 500 MiB per file, 1 GiB in total.
  • How long we keep it: until you delete the file in My Vault or delete your account. Deleting a file from My Vault doesn't delete the original on your phone.

Customer Support

  • How to reach us: in the app, Help & support → Contact Customer Support (signed in, with a verified email address). Replies appear in Help & support → My conversations. You can also email support@nextframestudio.online.
  • What a conversation stores: your message, your email address (so we can reply), and optionally your app version and device model. You can switch the app and device details off.
  • Screenshots: you can attach up to 3 screenshots per conversation, up to 10 MiB each. Only CreatorCue Customer Support can view them.
  • Reports: you can use "Report misuse" to report content, an account or misuse of CreatorCue. Reports are handled like support conversations: we reply and close them, and our handling is logged.
  • How long we keep it: conversations and their screenshots are deleted 12 months after their last activity, or when you delete your account.
  • If you email us instead, we receive your email address and what you choose to include.

Ads and consent

On the free plan, CreatorCue shows ads from Google AdMob. Its SDK may process device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics. Where required, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. Google's processing of ad data is described in Google's partner-site policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Permissions

  • Camera: to show the live preview and record when you choose to record.
  • Microphone: to record audio with your video.
  • Photos and videos: CreatorCue uses Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the items you select (for My Vault or support screenshots).

Service providers

We use these Google services to run CreatorCue. We share information only with them, as described here, or when the law requires it.

  • Firebase Authentication for sign-in and account security (Firebase privacy information).
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) in Sydney for the server data described above.
  • Google Play Billing for subscriptions and payments.
  • Google AdMob for ads on the free plan.

Records of administrator access

Every administrator action is recorded with what was done, when, the item concerned and, for My Vault, the reason. Records of access to your data are part of this. These records are kept for up to 12 months.

Server logs

Google Cloud keeps technical request logs, which can include IP addresses, for about 30 days.

How long we keep data

  • Account: until you delete it.
  • My Vault files: until you delete them or your account.
  • Support conversations and screenshots: 12 months after the last activity, or when you delete your account.
  • Recording allowance and Premium records: deleted automatically no more than 12 months after the last update. The purchase token is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Administrator access records: up to 12 months.
  • Server logs: about 30 days.
  • Scripts and videos on your phone: until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall it (videos in your Gallery are your files and are deleted separately).

Deleting your account

Use Settings → Delete account in the app, or the web page described on Delete your account. Deleting your account deletes:

  • your sign-in account;
  • your My Vault files;
  • your support conversations and screenshots;
  • your Premium link and purchase token.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

To stop the free allowance being claimed again by deleting and re-creating an account, we keep the scrambled key and monthly counts for up to 12 months. These records don't contain your email address.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing, request a copy, or withdraw consent. Write to support@nextframestudio.online, or use Help → Privacy & account help in the app. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder. Australian users can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

CreatorCue is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age for online services in their country. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, and our server checks each request before it reads or stores anything. On your phone, app data is kept in the app's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We post any change on this page with a new effective date.
