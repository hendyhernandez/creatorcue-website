CreatorCue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how the CreatorCue Android app handles information. CreatorCue is published on Google Play under the developer name NextFrame Studio, an individual developer based in Australia.

Contact: nextframestudio.id@gmail.com

Information we handle

  • Account details: If you sign in with email or Google, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, account identifier and, where provided, display name and profile image link.
  • Scripts and preferences: Scripts, prompt settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Recordings: Videos are recorded on your device and saved to its Movies/CreatorCue folder so they appear in your Gallery. We do not upload your videos.
  • Support: If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.
  • On your device only: your count of free recordings and the date it applies to, and the time Google Play last confirmed your Premium status (used only while Google Play can't be reached).

Permissions

Camera access is used to show the live preview and record video when you choose to record. Microphone access is used to capture audio during recording. CreatorCue does not access your existing photos or videos unless you choose to share them through Android.

Third-party services

Google Firebase Authentication

Firebase provides account sign-in and security. Google may process technical information such as IP address, app identity and sign-in events. See Firebase privacy information.

Google AdMob

The free plan may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Its SDK may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics. Where required, the app presents Google's consent choices before requesting ads. Premium subscribers do not receive ads. Learn more at Google's partner-site policy.

Google Play Billing

Google Play processes subscription payments. We do not receive your card or bank details. The app receives purchase status and related product information to determine whether Premium is active. To link a purchase to your CreatorCue account, the app gives Google Play a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of your account ID; it can't be turned back into your email address or name. Google handles payment records under its own policies.

How information is used and shared

We use account information to provide sign-in, secure accounts, associate subscription status and respond to support requests. We share information only with Google services described above, or when legally required. We do not sell personal information and do not operate our own application servers.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained until you delete your account. You can delete it in the app under Settings → Delete account, or request deletion by following the account deletion instructions. Web requests are completed within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it separately in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Scripts and settings remain on the device where they were created and are removed when you delete the app's data or uninstall it. Videos in your Gallery are your files and must be deleted separately. Google may retain billing and advertising information under its own policies.

Children

CreatorCue is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age for online services in their country. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing, request a copy or withdraw consent. Contact nextframestudio.id@gmail.com. Australian users may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Security and changes

We use Firebase authentication and encrypted connections for sign-in. Local app data is stored in the app's private storage. No method is completely secure. We may update this policy when our practices change and will update the effective date above.
