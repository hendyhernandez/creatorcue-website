The app

These terms apply to CreatorCue, published by NextFrame Studio, an individual developer based in Australia. CreatorCue is a teleprompter and camera app: you prepare scripts, display them while using the camera, and record videos to your phone. It also offers optional My Vault storage and in-app support. By using the app, you agree to these terms. Contact: nextframestudio.id@gmail.com.

Your account

Provide accurate sign-in information and keep your credentials secure. You must meet the minimum age for online services in your country, and in any event be at least 13. Recording on the free plan needs a verified email address. You may delete your account at any time in the app or on the web deletion page.

Free plan

  • Every teleprompter and camera feature.
  • Up to 5 saved scripts.
  • Up to 3 recordings per calendar month in your account's time zone, plus up to 3 bonus recordings a day from optional rewarded ads. A bonus lasts until the end of the month.
  • Ads are shown.

Free recordings need an internet connection and a verified email address. A recording that fails, or stops before any usable video is saved, is returned to your allowance.

CreatorCue Premium

  • Premium is an automatically renewing subscription sold through Google Play, with monthly, quarterly, semiannual and annual plans. It is billed by Google Play at the price shown in Google Play when you confirm, including applicable taxes.
  • Premium includes unlimited recordings, unlimited scripts and no ads, and lets you record offline for up to 72 hours after the last online check.
  • A subscription belongs to the CreatorCue account it was bought with and can't be transferred to another account or email address. To restore it on a new device, sign in with the same CreatorCue and Google Play accounts and use Settings → Restore purchases.
  • The subscription renews unless you cancel it in Google Play before the renewal date, subject to Google's billing terms. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; access generally continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Google Play handles payment failures, grace periods and refunds under its policies. Nothing here limits rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law or other mandatory consumer laws.
  • When Premium ends, the free plan's limits and ads apply again.

My Vault

  • Store only content you have the right to store.
  • Limits: 2,000 files, 500 MiB per file, 1 GiB in total.
  • Authorized administrators may view stored files only for support, security, reports or legal obligations, as described in the Privacy Policy.
