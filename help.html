Help & support

Answers to common questions about CreatorCue. If you need more help, write to the developer.

Contact the developer

  • Questions and feedback: nextframestudio.id@gmail.com
  • Report a problem: email us with your phone model, Android version and what happened. Please don't send photos or videos unless we ask and you're comfortable sharing them.
  • Subscription & billing: payments, refunds and cancellations are handled by Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). We can help with anything about Premium in the app.
  • Privacy & account: see the Privacy Policy and account deletion.

Frequently asked questions

How many free recordings do I get?

The free plan includes 3 successful recordings a day. The count resets at midnight in your phone's time zone. Recordings that fail, or that are interrupted before 5 seconds of video is saved, aren't counted.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited recordings, unlimited scripts (the free plan keeps up to 5) and no ads. Premium is a subscription bought through Google Play.

I changed phones. How do I get Premium back?

Sign in with the same CreatorCue account and the same Google Play account, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

Can I move Premium to another account or email?

No. A subscription bought in the app is linked to the CreatorCue account you were signed in with and can only be restored to that account.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting your CreatorCue account doesn't cancel the subscription.

Can I pause while recording?

Yes. Tap Pause while recording and Resume to continue. The take is saved as one video, and the prompt stays where you paused.

Where are my videos saved?

In your Gallery, in the Movies/CreatorCue folder. Videos stay on your phone; CreatorCue doesn't upload them.

Are my scripts uploaded?

No. Scripts are stored only on your device. Use Settings → Backup & restore to save a copy where you choose.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Without the app: see Delete account.

Using the teleprompter

  1. Create or open a script and tap Record.
  2. In the camera, open the settings icon to choose the countdown, camera, quality, prompt speed, text size and position.
  3. Use the Play/Pause script button to start or stop the scrolling; tap the screen to show or hide the controls.
  4. Tap Record to start, Pause/Resume as needed, and Stop to save the take to your Gallery.
