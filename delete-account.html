Delete your account

Delete your CreatorCue account in the app or on the web, at any time. Here's exactly what that removes.

Three ways to delete

In the app

Open CreatorCue and go to Settings → Delete account. You may be asked to sign in again.

On the web

No app needed. Sign in with the account you want to delete and confirm.

Open the deletion page

By email

Only if you can't sign in. Write from the address on the account.

Email a request

Delete on the web

  1. Go to creatorcue-46aad.web.app/delete-account, the CreatorCue account deletion page.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete (Google, or email and password). If you signed in more than a few minutes ago, you'll be asked to sign in again.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and select Delete account.
  4. The deletion runs immediately. Your server data is deleted first and your sign-in account last.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • Your My Vault files.
  • Your support conversations and their screenshots.
  • Your Premium link and purchase token.
  • When you delete in the app: the scripts and settings on that phone.

What is kept, and for how long

To stop the free recording allowance being claimed again by deleting and re-creating accounts, we keep scrambled anti-abuse records: monthly recording and bonus counts, account dates and time zone, scrambled device identifiers, and a note that a Premium purchase was released (without the purchase token).

These records don't contain your email address, and they're deleted automatically within 12 months of their last update.

Not affected

  • Your Google Play subscription. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Videos in your Gallery and the originals of files you added to My Vault. They're your files on your phone; delete them in your Gallery app.
  • Scripts on other phones. They're never uploaded. Uninstall CreatorCue or clear its storage on those phones.
  • Google's own records, such as Google Play payment history and advertising data, which Google handles under its policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

If you can't sign in

Email nextframestudio.id@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject "Delete my CreatorCue account". We'll verify that the request comes from the account holder and help you get back into the account so the deletion can be completed. See the Privacy Policy for details.
