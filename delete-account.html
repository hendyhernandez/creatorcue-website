Delete your CreatorCue account

CreatorCue is published by NextFrame Studio, an individual developer in Australia. You can delete your account from the app or request deletion without the app.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open CreatorCue and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the request. You may be asked to re-authenticate for security.

Your Firebase account is deleted and scripts and settings stored on that device are removed.

Option 2 — without the app

Send an email from the address associated with your CreatorCue account with the subject “Delete my CreatorCue account”. We may ask you to verify ownership. We complete verified web deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your CreatorCue account information held in Firebase Authentication, including email, name, profile link, sign-in methods and account ID.
  • When deletion is performed in the app, local scripts, prompt settings and preferences on that device.

What is not deleted

  • Local scripts on other devices, because they are not uploaded to us. Uninstall CreatorCue or clear its app storage on those devices.
  • Videos saved in your Gallery. Delete them separately in your Gallery app.
  • Google Play purchase and payment records, which Google retains under its policies. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob, which is handled under Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
