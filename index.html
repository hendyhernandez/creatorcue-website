Stay on script without looking away

CreatorCue puts your words on the camera screen, close to the lens, and records while you read.

Smart teleprompter

Write or paste your script, then open the camera. The script scrolls in a prompt box over the live preview, so you can read naturally and keep your eyes near the lens.

Tune it until it reads like you speak: change the pace, the look of the text, and how each line arrives.

  • Smooth scrolling or phrase by phrasePhrases fade or slide in
  • Adjustable scrolling speedPause and resume the script any time
  • Text size, colour and line spacingPlus text opacity
  • Outline, shadow and backgroundReadable over any scene
  • Prompt box sizeSet its width and height
  • Reading-line markerMarks the line to read
  • Mirrored textFor beam-splitter teleprompter glass
  • Countdown before recordingTime to settle in

Video recording

Record straight from the prompter in 720p, 1080p or 4K, where your phone's camera supports it. Finished takes are saved to your phone's Gallery.

Front and rear camera

Film yourself with the front camera or switch to the rear camera. The script stays on screen either way.

Recording controls and preview

Pause and resume a take, or pause only the script. Stop and save when you're done, then watch the take on the review screen and keep it or delete it.

My Vault

Private cloud storage for the photos and videos you choose. Files are kept in your account on Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia, and other users can't see them. Up to 2,000 files, 500 MiB each, 1 GiB in total.

Scripts on your phone

Write and edit scripts in the app. They stay on your phone, and you can back them up to a file you choose and restore them later.

Help built in

Ask a question from Help & support, attach up to 3 screenshots, and read the reply in the app. Use Report misuse to flag a problem.

About CreatorCue

CreatorCue is a teleprompter camera app for Android, made by NextFrame Studio, an individual developer based in Australia. It's for anyone who records to camera — short videos, presentations, lessons — and wants to stay on script while looking at the lens.

Questions

What is CreatorCue?

A teleprompter and camera in one Android app. You write a script, read it on screen while you record, and save the video to your phone. Optional My Vault storage keeps chosen photos and videos in your account.

How does the teleprompter work?

Open a script and tap Record. The script scrolls in a prompt box over the camera preview. From the camera settings you can change the scrolling speed, switch between smooth scrolling and phrase by phrase, adjust text size and colour, mirror the text for teleprompter glass, and set a countdown. Pause the script whenever you need a moment.

How does video recording work?

Choose the front or rear camera and a quality your phone supports (720p, 1080p or 4K), then tap Record. You can pause and resume, and Stop & save ends the take. The video is saved to your phone's Gallery and opens on a review screen.

On the free plan, recording needs an internet connection and a verified email address, and each take counts toward your monthly allowance. A take that fails before any usable video is saved doesn't count.

What is My Vault?

Optional private cloud storage in your CreatorCue account. You pick photos and videos with Android's photo picker, and only those files are uploaded; CreatorCue doesn't get access to your other photos. Each account can store up to 2,000 files, up to 500 MiB per file and 1 GiB in total. Files stay until you delete them in My Vault or delete your account. Deleting a file from My Vault doesn't delete the original on your phone.

How is private media protected?
  • Files are stored in your account on Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia, and are sent over encrypted connections.
  • Other users can't see your files.
  • Authorized CreatorCue administrators can view a file only to provide technical support, keep CreatorCue secure, handle reports, or meet legal obligations. Every view is logged with its reason, and administrators can't change or delete your files.
  • Your files and messages aren't used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What is Premium?

A Google Play subscription that gives unlimited recordings and saved scripts, removes ads, and lets you record offline for up to 72 hours after the last online check. It comes in 1, 3, 6 and 12-month periods. Premium will be available once CreatorCue is on Google Play. See Free and Premium.

Is CreatorCue on Google Play yet?

Not yet. The app is in final testing. This site will link to the listing once it's published.

How can I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Without the app, use the web deletion page described on Delete your account, which also lists what is deleted and what is kept.

How can I contact support?

In the app, open Help & support → Contact Customer Support. You can also email support@nextframestudio.online.
